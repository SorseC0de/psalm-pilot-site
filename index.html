Privacy Policy

Psalm Pilot collects nothing. That is the whole of it, but here are the specifics.

  • No data leaves your device. The app makes no network requests of its own. There is no server, no account, and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your reading stays with you. Bookmarks, notes, reminders, game scores, and your chosen translation and typeface are stored on the device, and are readable only by this app. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Reminders are local. A psalm you schedule is delivered by iOS on the device itself. Nothing about it is transmitted anywhere.
  • Donations go through Apple. If you choose to give, the purchase is handled entirely by the App Store. Apple — not this app — receives the payment details, and no payment information is ever visible to or stored by Psalm Pilot.
  • Children. Since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date below. Questions about it can go to the same address as support.
